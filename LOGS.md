# LOGS.md: Timeline

One entry per closed cycle, appended by the planner at close under `POLARIS.md` section 6.5. Oldest first, newest at the end. Entries are never edited. Each entry says what happened, never how it works: the explanation of the last cycle is in `DEBRIEF.md`, and the design of the system is in `SYSTEM.md`.

At most three bullets per entry. A part of the third bullet that does not apply is omitted, and the whole bullet is omitted when none applies.

```markdown
## Cycle <N>, <title> (<YYYY-MM-DD>)

- <One or two lines: what the system can do now that it could not before.>
- <Revisions and blocks, each with its cause in a few words, or `Closed on the first revision.`>
- <Milestone completed, amendments applied, `LATER.md` IDs resolved.>
```
